How to Use gt-transformer

1. Input Rules

  • Aligned FASTA: 5 to 500 sequences, identical aligned length up to 50,000 bp (max 20 MB upload).
  • Alphabet: Standard IUPAC nucleotide codes (ACGTUNRYKMSWBDHV-.?) and gaps (-, .). Whitespace, \r, and embedded digits are stripped; sequences are uppercased automatically.
  • Headers: First word after > is used as the display name; hover any sequence name to see the full header.

2. Output Columns

  • BP (orig) & Span (orig): 1-based alignment column of the breakpoint and its confidence interval (start..end bp).
  • Carriers: Recombinant sequence(s) carrying the mosaic switch (e.g. R1_rec).
  • Ext Parents (L→R): Flanking parental lineages across the breakpoint (backbone/major vs donor/minor parent). Identical sibling candidates are reported as P1 or P5.

3. Modes & Checkpoints

  • Hybrid (default): Combines permutation-calibrated flanking KL scan with 4-row quartet Transformer scoring.
  • Transformer only / Flanking KL only: Run the neural quartet scorer standalone or the fast analytical KL scan alone.
  • Checkpoints: ckpt_N40000 (40k corpus standard model, default) and ckpt_N10000 (10k standard model); switch to ckpt_N10000_extreme for high-divergence OOD alignments, ckpt_N10000_v2 for LD-invariant features, or ckpt_ensemble for multi-model LogMeanExp fusion.

4. Known Limits & Run Times

  • Limits: ~30 bp edge dead zone at alignment ends; short tracts (<100 bp) or <10 parsimony-informative sites have reduced power.
  • Typical CPU run times (from timings.csv): 8 × 1,500 bp: ~1.2 s  |  6 × 1,200 bp: ~2.1 s  |  60 × 3,000 bp: ~6.4 s  |  96 × 3,536 bp: ~23.8 s.
