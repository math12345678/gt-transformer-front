Privacy Policy

GT-Transformer Recombination Workbench · Effective Date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GT-Transformer Recombination Workbench ("the Service") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our web application for nucleotide sequence recombination analysis.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: If you create a local account, we store your chosen username and a salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hash of your password (we never store plaintext passwords).
  • Google Sign-In Information: If you sign in with Google, we request only basic identity scopes (openid, email, and profile). We store your Google account subject identifier, email address, and display name solely to authenticate your session and keep your analysis runs private to your account.
  • Uploaded Alignments & Analysis Runs: FASTA alignments, optional ground-truth files, and inference results that you submit while signed in are stored in the application's database so you can view, compare, export, or delete your saved runs.
  • Session Cookies: We use an HTTP-only session cookie strictly to maintain your logged-in state. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party behavioral tracking.

2. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate your account and isolate your private analysis history from other users.
  • To run recombination breakpoint inference and generate diagnostic plots, tables, and downloadable reports.
  • Optional AI Assistant: If you explicitly ask a question in the in-app AI Assistant, a compact structured summary of your active run's detected events (excluding raw full-genome alignment arrays) is sent to the configured AI provider solely to answer your question.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or sequence data to third parties. Google user data received through Google Sign-In is used exclusively for authentication within the Service and is not shared with any third party.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You may delete any of your saved analysis runs at any time directly inside the application. You may also request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting the administrator below.

5. Security

We apply standard web security protections including salted password hashing, HTTP-only session cookies, CSRF state verification for OAuth flows, and strict Content-Security-Policy and security headers.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the maintainer at mathlete.world@gmail.com.
